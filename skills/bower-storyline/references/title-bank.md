# Action Title Bank — the calibration corpus

Load this whole file whenever composing or judging titles. Rules constrain; **this locates the
target.** Tags: [REAL] verbatim from actual McKinsey/BCG/Accenture decks (USPS engagement) ·
[BOOK] from the reference literature · [SKILL] from prior-art skills · [DERIVED] constructed.

## The house register

Plain, specific, complete sentences. **Long is fine** — several real McKinsey titles run past
15 words. No metaphor, no sparkle, no verb theatrics. A strong title reads like a finding
stated by a careful analyst to a skeptical partner, not a headline written by a marketer.
Write it the way you would say it, then delete the hedges. Real firm titles do not try to be
interesting; they try to be exact — the interest comes from the claim being true and mattering.

## Vague verb → end product (Minto)

| What was said | What was meant |
|---|---|
| Strengthen regional effectiveness | Assign planning responsibility to the regions |
| Reduce accounts receivable | Establish a system for following up overdue accounts |
| Review management processes | Determine whether management processes need to be revised |
| Improve financial reporting | Install a system that gives early notice of change |
| Tackle strategic issues | Define a clear long-term strategy |
| Redeploy manpower resources | Place people in positions of comparable responsibility |

## Real deck titles, verbatim [REAL]

McKinsey: "USPS is experiencing unprecedented losses" · "Losses have been driven by volume
declines, RHB pre-funding requirements, and limitations on cost savings" · "The 'Actions within
Postal Service control' case includes product and service initiatives above the baseline to
grow volume".
Accenture: "Lower cost competitors are gaining share from legacy postal operators in liberalized
markets…" · "…as the elimination of the postal monopoly is likely to exacerbate the structural
decline of mail volumes" · "The USPS Is Under-Diversified Relative to Global Benchmarks".

**Note the register:** plainer and longer than skill-generated titles tend to be. Specific,
complete, one assertion. No metaphors. Several run well past ten words.

Presentation-level [REAL]: "Sustaining Quality and Operational Excellence" · "Capturing the
Full Electricity Efficiency Potential of the U.K." · "Restoring the USPS to Profitability by
2020" (the SMART repair of the weak "USPS Future Business Model").

## Governing thoughts

**Bad:** We should think about international expansion (vague) · Our growth strategy (topic) ·
Customers want more features (no action implication) · Offer $500,000 for a share of the Swell
Boards start-up (no owner, stake, why, horizon) · We should take steps to meet the new
standards (obvious).

**Good:** SurfCo should offer $500,000 for 51% of the Swell Boards start-up to regain market
share from competitors within the next two years [BOOK] · The decline in Q3 margin is caused by
raw material costs, not pricing — we should hedge, not raise prices [SKILL] · We can hit our
2027 EBITDA target by capturing three identified cost-out opportunities worth $42M [SKILL] ·
Pioneer needs to acquire Shanghai Bank and invest $60 million in building capabilities to
establish a winning position [BOOK].

## Complete ladders — titles are only assessable in sequence

**A. Passing [SKILL]:**
```
S04  Deposit growth stalled at 2% while funding costs rose 60bps
        but ↓
S05  NIM compresses ~35bps by Q4 unless the mix shifts
        but ↓
S06  CASA campaigns alone recovered only a third of past gaps
        therefore ↓
S07  Repricing the top decile via RBPE protects ~20bps at low attrition risk
```

**A′. Same content, failing — what per-slide generation produces:**
```
S04 Deposit Overview → S05 NIM Trends → S06 CASA Campaign Results → S07 RBPE Proposal
```
Each defensible alone; the sequence argues nothing. Every junction is "and then".

**B. Question chain, real deck [REAL]:**
```
S02  USPS is experiencing unprecedented losses          → reader asks "why?"
S03  Losses have been driven by volume declines, RHB pre-funding requirements,
     and limitations on cost savings                    → "impact of each?" → waterfall
```

**C. To B or Not to B, discipline all the way down [BOOK]:**
```
GOV  Coonawarra Corp should invest in Black's intelligent cloud-based storage solution
 ├─ We investigated four options to deliver intelligent cloud-based data storage capabilities
 │    ├─ Option 1 – Outsourcing to Yellow Company is possible but risky
 │    ├─ Option 2 – Purchasing tools from Pink Corporation is overly complicated
 │    ├─ Option 3 – Insourcing to our internal IT team is possible, but expensive and risky
 │    └─ Option 4 – Outsourcing to Black Inc. meets all criteria
 ├─ Of the four options, Black Inc. provides the best solution for our needs
 └─ As a result, we recommend a 10-week program to initiate the migration to Black
```
Even option sub-headings are verdicts, not labels.

**D. Deductive So-whats by pattern [DERIVED]** (lender, cost-to-income drifting):
- *Houston:* "Cost-to-income reaches 62% by FY27 unless we consolidate origination;
  consolidating recovers 7pts for a $9M one-off"
- *Watch Out:* "Protecting the FY27 margin plan needs three risk actions approved this quarter"
- *Close the Gap:* "Meeting the FY27 55% cost-to-income target needs $14M of the $22M
  identified cost-out"

The deductive So-what carries **both the action and its reason** — a pattern requirement.

**E. Parallel grammar in a grouping [BOOK]** (Traffic Light, verbal):
- We are now successfully prioritising maintenance tasks according to the impact on production
  rather than the time when the task was logged
- We are continuing to improve communication between shift supervisors and the maintenance team
- We are working towards operators telling supervisors as soon as they see a potential problem

All three "We are [verb]-ing" + a behaviour change + its object. That parallelism is Minto's
same-kind rule made visible.

## Anti-pattern gallery — professional-looking, empty

Key Considerations for the Transformation (blank) · Strategic Priorities Overview (topic+blank)
· We Conducted 15 Stakeholder Interviews (process) · Managing Risk Is Important for Success
(general truth) · All Divisions Fail to Follow Best Practices (absolute) · Significant Pressure
on Margins (unquantified) · Unlocking Value Across the Portfolio (slop) · Our Journey to
Operational Excellence (slop, no claim) · Revenue Grew 23% and Costs Rose 8% and Headcount Fell
4% (compound) · It Might Be Worth Considering an Exit (no recommendation) · Q3 Results (topic)
· Next Steps (topic — extremely common, still wrong).

## Exempt slides — do not force titles onto

Cover · section dividers (label + the Key Line sentence as the promise) · agenda (short items,
deliberately ignorable; start items with action verbs) · appendix separators · quote slides.
Everything else gets an action title. No exception for "the chart explains it".
