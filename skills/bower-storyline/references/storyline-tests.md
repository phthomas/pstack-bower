# Storyline Tests — run in this order

A failure upstream invalidates everything below it. Fixes route upstream.

## 1. Purpose (before anything)
"As a result of receiving my communication, I want my audience to ___." Must name an audience
action, not a speaker wish ("I want to update leadership" fails).

## 2. Introduction — CTQ, six checks
1. Audience nods at the Context — nothing to argue, nothing needing proof.
2. Trigger states what *changed* (threat OR opportunity), tightly linked to Context.
3. Exactly ONE question flows from C+T — and it is the question the deck answers.
4. The question is framed from the audience's side ("why should this prospect partner with
   us?", never "why should we partner with them?").
5. The Answer answers that question, precisely.
6. Someone not in the room could follow it.

## 3. The So what — five rules
1. Answers the audience's question — tight connection.
2. Unifies the whole story (forces culling of disconnected ideas).
3. One idea, one sentence — no conjunction smuggling in a second claim.
4. Synthesises or summarises the supporting ideas.
5. Powerful and supportable — a CEO hearing it must not reply "…so what should we do?" and must
   not find it obvious.

## 4. Pattern fit
Named, with a stated reason. Honestly fits — no force-fitting. Family matches the need
(one of why/how/what → grouping; why AND how → deductive).

## 5. Structure
**Grouping (5):** MECE together · each idea provokes and answers exactly one audience question ·
each synthesises (not just summarises) what's below · each parents 2–5 children · each adds
genuine value (no "look how much work we did").
**Deductive (4):** only one possible recommendation follows · statement/comment/therefore each
does its job, comment TIGHT to statement · each parents a MECE grouping, never another chain ·
no padding.

## 6. Pyramid — three tests
- **Down:** each parent poses ONE question (why OR how, not both) answered completely below.
- **Across:** each level MECE — nameable with one plural noun.
- **Up:** each group yields one so-what that IS the parent.

## 7. The ladder — three tests, increasing strength
- **Memo read:** titles alone, top to bottom, argue without any slide bodies.
- **Connective:** between every pair, "therefore" or "but" — never "and then".
- **Question chain (diagnostic):** each title triggers a question the NEXT title answers. When
  this fails you know what the missing slide must say.

## 8. Per-slide
One message (the title IS the summary) · body will be able to prove the title · title passes
the classifier (see title-craft.md defect vocabulary) · every FACT tag has a [D#]/data home ·
no orphans (every S## row maps to a Key Line node) · no repeated or contradictory titles.

## Scoring discipline
Anything failing 1–4 stops the compile — do not proceed to slides on a broken frame. 5–8
failures are fix-and-rerun. When in doubt whether a title defect is wording or thinking:
blank assertions, orphans and non-sequiturs are THINKING defects — route upstream.
